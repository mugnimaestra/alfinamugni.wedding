# Gallery Upload System - User Guide

## 📸 How to Upload Photos

Welcome! This guide will help you upload and share your wedding photos.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Navigate to Gallery
1. Open the wedding website
2. Scroll to the **Gallery** section
3. Click **"Lihat & Unggah Foto"** button
4. OR navigate directly to `/gallery`

---

## Upload Methods

### Method 1: Drag & Drop
1. Click on the **Upload** tab
2. Drag photo files from your computer
3. Drop them into the upload zone
4. Photos will be automatically processed

### Method 2: Browse Files
1. Click **"Browse Files"** button
2. Select one or multiple photos
3. Click **Open**
4. Photos will begin processing

### Method 3: Camera Capture (Mobile)
1. Click **"Take Photo"** button
2. Allow camera access when prompted
3. Take a photo
4. Photo will be automatically added

---

## File Requirements

### Supported Formats
- ✅ JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg)
- ✅ PNG (.png)
- ✅ WebP (.webp)
- ✅ HEIC (.heic) - iPhone photos

### File Limits
- **Maximum file size**: 20 MB per photo
- **Maximum files per batch**: 50 photos
- **Minimum resolution**: No minimum (but larger is better!)

## Upload Process

### Step 1: Select Photos
- Choose photos from your device
- Multiple photos can be uploaded at once
- Preview grid shows selected photos

### Step 2: Auto-Processing
The system automatically:
- ✅ Compresses images based on your network speed
- ✅ Generates thumbnails
- ✅ Optimizes for mobile viewing
- ✅ Preserves photo quality

### Step 3: Organize (Optional)
Before uploading, you can:
- **Categorize** photos:
  - Ceremony
  - Reception
  - Pre-wedding
  - Family
  - Friends
  - Candid
  - Traditional

### Step 4: Upload
1. Click **"Upload All"** button
2. Wait for progress bar to complete
3. ✅ Success message appears
4. Photos will be reviewed before public display

## Understanding Compression

### Why Compress?
- Faster uploads, especially on mobile data
- Reduces data usage
- Better viewing experience for all guests

### Network Adaptation

#### 📶 4G / Good WiFi
- **Quality**: High (85%)
- **Processing**: Fast
- **File size**: ~800 KB per photo
- **Perfect for**: High-quality uploads

#### 📶 3G / Moderate WiFi
- **Quality**: Medium (75%)
- **Processing**: Moderate
- **File size**: ~500 KB per photo
- **Best for**: Balanced speed and quality

#### 📶 2G / Slow Connection
- **Quality**: Lower (60%)
- **Processing**: Slower
- **File size**: ~200 KB per photo
- **Recommended for**: Emergency uploads only

### Network Indicator
The upload page shows:
- 📶 Your carrier name
- 🔴/🟡/🟢 Network condition
- Current speed (Mbps)
- Time of day indicator

## Tips for Best Results

### 📱 Mobile Users
1. ✅ Use WiFi when available
2. ✅ Upload during off-peak hours (10 PM - 6 AM)
3. ✅ Close other apps to free memory
4. ✅ Ensure 20%+ battery before large uploads

### 💻 Desktop Users
1. ✅ Use wired connection for fastest speeds
2. ✅ Upload multiple photos at once (batch)
3. ✅ Keep browser tab active during upload

### 📸 Photo Quality
1. ✅ Use the highest resolution your camera supports
2. ✅ Ensure good lighting in photos
3. ✅ Avoid blurry or dark photos
4. ✅ Portrait orientation recommended for people photos

## Troubleshooting

### ❌ Upload Fails
**Possible Causes**:
- Poor internet connection
- File too large (> 20 MB)
- Unsupported format

**Solutions**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try uploading one photo at a time
3. Convert HEIC to JPEG on iPhone (Settings → Camera → Formats)
4. Refresh the page and try again

### ⏳ Upload Takes Too Long
**Solutions**:
1. Switch to WiFi if on mobile data
2. Reduce number of photos per batch
3. Upload during off-peak hours
4. Enable Auto-Compress in Settings

### 🖼️ Photo Not Appearing
**Note**: All photos are reviewed before appearing publicly.
- Photos typically approved within 24 hours
- Check back later to see your photos in the gallery

### 📱 Camera Not Working
**Solutions**:
1. Allow camera access in browser settings
2. Ensure HTTPS connection (not HTTP)
3. Try using "Browse Files" instead

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How many photos can I upload?
**A**: Up to 50 photos at once, unlimited total uploads.

### Q: Will my photos be compressed?
**A**: Yes, but quality is preserved. We use smart compression that adapts to your network speed.

### Q: Can I upload videos?
**A**: Currently, only photos are supported.

### Q: How long until my photo appears?
**A**: Photos are reviewed within 24 hours before appearing publicly.

### Q: Can I delete my photos?
**A**: Contact the wedding couple to request removal.

### Q: Do I need to create an account?
**A**: No! Upload is open to all wedding guests.

### Q: What if my phone camera photos are HEIC?
**A**: iPhone users: Go to Settings → Camera → Formats → Choose "Most Compatible" to save as JPEG instead.
